# What's new in DrivePanel (web and server)

<!-- Newest version on top. Same versions and order as NOVEDADES.md (Spanish). -->

## 1.0.15
- **Forgot your username or password?** Every account now has a **recovery code**. It's shown
  once when the account is created (if your account already existed, the next time you sign in).
  You can copy it, send it to yourself on WhatsApp, Telegram or email, or download it as a file.
- If you forget your username or password, tap «Forgot your username or password?» on the sign-in
  screen, enter the code and a new password, and you'll also see your username.
- If you lose it, get a new one from your photo menu → «Recovery code».
- The administrator can still set a new password for anyone.

## 1.0.14
- **What's new notice:** after DrivePanel is updated, the first time you sign in a window shows you
  what has changed. You can read it again any time from your photo menu → "What's new", or by
  tapping the version at the bottom of the side menu.

## 1.0.13
- **Quick account switching:** tap your photo at the top right to see the other accounts signed in
  on this device; tap one to switch to it without typing the password. "Add another account" signs
  in to a new one (that time with its password). When you sign out, the sign-in screen offers the
  remembered accounts, and the ✕ removes one from the device.

## 1.0.12
- **Web shortcut different from the app:** if you install DrivePanel from the browser it is called
  "DrivePanel Web" and has a green globe icon, so you don't mix it up with the app.
- Downloading a folder as it is works in Chrome and Edge even when you open it with `http://` and the IP.

## 1.0.11
- **More documents:** open and edit `.xls`, `.ods` and `.odt` too; and view `.doc`, `.rtf`, `.pptx`,
  `.odp` and Access databases.
- **Search in the folder:** search inside the folder you have open, and "Search everywhere" looks in
  your whole drive.
- **Download folders without .zip:** choose "As it is, with its subfolders" and the files are
  downloaded one by one with their folders.
- After an update, the browser and the app always load the new version of the web.

## 1.0.10
- **Documents inside DrivePanel:** view and edit PDF (and sign them), Word and Excel without
  downloading them. From "Actions" you can create a new Word document or spreadsheet.

## 1.0.9
- **Spanish or English:** choose the language in Settings or on the sign-in screen. The first time
  it uses the browser's language. The Android app follows the same language.

## 1.0.8
- **Disk full warning:** when the disk is filling up a warning appears at the top of the web (and on
  the phone with the app). The percentages are chosen in Settings, and it can also warn you on Telegram.

## 1.0.7
- **Photos:** choose which folders they come from, view them by date or by album, and they load
  little by little so it stays fast even with thousands.

## 1.0.6
- **Watch videos and listen to music without downloading them.** Videos resume where you left off.

## 1.0.5
- **Share on WhatsApp and Telegram** the link to a file or folder, with its preview.

## 1.0.4
- Your photo menu has an entry to go to **Settings**.

## 1.0.3
- **General settings:** machine profile (Raspberry Pi or PC) and panel colour.
- **Upload graph** like the Windows 11 one, with live speed.
- On the phone, when you open a shared link you can choose "Open in the DrivePanel app" or
  "Download here".

## 1.0.2
- When you sign in, the **Activity panel** opens. If you prefer My Drive, change it in your photo
  menu → "Home screen".
